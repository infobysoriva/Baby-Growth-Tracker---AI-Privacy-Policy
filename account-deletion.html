Account Deletion

You can delete your Baby Growth Tracker – AI account at any time.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Baby Growth Tracker – AI app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Select Delete Account
  4. Confirm deletion

Once deleted, all personal data including baby profiles, growth data, photos, and caregiver access will be permanently removed.
