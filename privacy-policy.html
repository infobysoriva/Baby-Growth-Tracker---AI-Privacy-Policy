Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Baby Growth Tracker – AI (“we”, “our”, “the App”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Baby profile information (name/nickname, date of birth, gender)
  • Growth data (height, weight, head circumference)
  • Sleep, feeding, diaper, activity, milestone records
  • Notes, photos, and optional AI-related inputs
  • Account information (email address, authentication ID)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide core baby tracking features
  • Synchronize data across caregivers
  • Improve performance and user experience
  • Enable AI-powered insights
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Data may be shared only with invited caregivers, trusted providers (hosting/auth/analytics/AI processing), or when required by law.
