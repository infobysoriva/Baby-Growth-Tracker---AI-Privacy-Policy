Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Baby Growth Tracker – AI (“we”, “our”, “the App”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Baby profile information (name or nickname, date of birth, gender)
  • Growth data (height, weight, head circumference)
  • Sleep, feeding, diaper, activity, and milestone records
  • Notes and optional AI-related inputs
  • Account information (email address, authentication ID)

Profile Images & Photos

If you choose to upload or take a photo to use as a profile or avatar image, we collect and store that image only for display purposes within the app. Uploading a photo is optional and initiated by the user.

2. Camera & Gallery Access

The app may request access to your device camera or photo gallery solely to allow users to upload profile or avatar images. Images are not used for facial recognition, identity verification, advertising, or training AI models.

All images related to children are uploaded and managed exclusively by parents or legal caregivers.

3. Notifications

Baby Growth Tracker – AI may send push notifications to remind you about baby-related activities such as feeding, sleep tracking, growth updates, milestones, or app-related information.

Notifications are optional and can be enabled or disabled at any time through your device settings.

4. Caregivers & Shared Access

The app allows parents or legal caregivers to invite other caregivers (such as another parent or guardian) to access the same baby profile.

Shared caregivers can view and manage baby-related data based on permissions granted by the account owner. We do not share baby data with anyone unless explicitly invited by the primary caregiver.

5. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide core baby tracking functionality
  • Synchronize data securely across caregivers
  • Display user-selected profile or avatar images
  • Send optional reminders and notifications
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Generate AI-powered insights (growth trends and predictions)
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse

6. Data Storage & Security

Data is securely stored using industry-standard encryption and protected backend infrastructure. Access to baby data is restricted to authorized caregivers only.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

Data may be shared only:

  • With caregivers you explicitly invite
  • With trusted service providers (authentication, hosting, storage, analytics)
  • When required by law

8. Children’s Privacy

Baby Growth Tracker – AI is intended for parents and caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data only while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app or by contacting us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
